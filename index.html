Sound familiar?

The loan lands. Then it disappears.

Your maintenance loan arrives once a term, so it feels like a lot of money until suddenly it isn't. Spenvo spreads it out week by week, so the end of term doesn't arrive before your next payment.

Week 1

“I'm rich.”

Freshers, takeaways, a new coat. It all seems affordable.

Week 6

“Wait, where did it go?”

Your balance is half what you expected, and rent is due.

Week 10

“Beans again.”

Weeks of term left and nothing left in the account.

What Spenvo does

A budget that actually tells you what you can spend.

A weekly budget, not a monthly guess

Spenvo splits what you have left into a budget for each week of the month. Overspend one week and the rest of the month adjusts automatically. Get paid and your budget goes up straight away.
